About the Game

A relaxing merge
experience with
colorful cafe
progression

The whole game's built around these really zen gameplay moments - you merge stuff, get cool upgrades, and honestly, watching those fruits combine is weirdly satisfying. You'll be mixing different fruits to create new recipes, and slowly but surely, your cafe starts looking exactly how you want it.

We wanted something that'd actually help you unwind after a long day, you know? The animations are smooth and there's always something new to unlock, so you'll probably find yourself coming back to it.
